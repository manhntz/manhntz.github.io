Privacy Policy

YT Uploader — last updated September 27, 2026

What the tool does

YT Uploader is a command-line application that uploads videos to YouTube channels owned by the person who runs it. It accesses the YouTube Data API on behalf of that person after they sign in with Google.

Google user data we access

When you sign in, the tool requests the following YouTube scopes: youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube (to set thumbnails, add videos to playlists and confirm which channel is signed in). It reads only your channel's name, handle and ID, and writes only the videos and metadata you choose to upload.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored locally on your own computer, in a file that only you control. Video files, titles, descriptions and upload logs also stay on your computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any Google user data to third parties. The tool has no backend, no analytics and no advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the token file on your computer.

Limited Use

YT Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
